Spaghetti

Description

This spaghetti recipe is a classic Italian dish and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz of spaghetti
  • Marinara sauce
  • Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add salt.
  2. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  3. Drain the spaghetti and return it to the pot.
  4. Add marinara sauce to the spaghetti and mix well.
  5. Serve the spaghetti on a plate and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese.
